AI Coach

Chat with your data.

The AI Coach is an optional, read-only chat that sees your history, measurements, routines and goals. It uses tools to pull details on demand and answer with real context — never inventing numbers.

  • ▸ 13 read tools: workouts, PRs, volume, trends, measurements, sleep, cardio and goals
  • ▸ Multi-provider: OpenAI, OpenRouter, Ollama or any compatible endpoint
  • ▸ Local tokens: stored with flutter_secure_storage, never in the database
  • ▸ Routine proposals: AI suggests changes and you approve before applying
