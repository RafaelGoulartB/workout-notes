## App name
Workout Notes

## Short description
Dark workout journal for sets, routines, progress and body stats.

## Full description
Workout Notes is a complete workout journal designed for fast session logging and clear progress tracking.

Log exercises, sets, weight, reps, distance, time, RPE and notes during your workout. Build reusable routines, review history on a calendar, follow volume and frequency charts, track body measurements and export your data whenever needed.

Main features:
- Active workout logging with workout and rest timers
- Exercise library organized by muscle group and energy system
- Quick Add for fast set logging
- Reusable routines for faster sessions
- Progress charts for volume, frequency, cardio and personal records
- Workout calendar and completed session history
- Body measurements, photos and trends
- Complete JSON backup and CSV export
- Light/dark theme with customizable accent colors
